Profiling Report – `profiling.ts`

Tested inputs: 10, 10³, 10⁶ numbers

Summary

| Input Size | Total Time | Bottlenecks                      |
|------------|------------|----------------------------------|
| 10         | ~6 ms      | None                             |
| 10³        | ~10 ms     | `tokenize()`, `parse()`          |
| 10⁶        | ~622 ms    | `std()`, `map()`, `parseFloat()` |

Notes

- Execution time grows with input size.
- Largest cost: `Executor.execute()` with `parseFloat()` inside `map()`.
- `parse()` becomes slower due to recursion on long expressions.

Optimization Tips

- Replace `map(parseFloat)` with a more efficient loop.
- Avoid deep recursion in parser (consider iterative approach).
- Pre-process input numbers more efficiently.
